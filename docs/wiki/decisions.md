- **ADR-253** (2026-08-17) — **K11b/K11c boundaries: screen keys on the WINDOW; audio admits what it cannot obtain.** Both built before any grabber or audio backend exists (the K10 gate), pure and re-distilled server-side, with no output type able to express raw capture. **Screen**: a frame has no natural sensitive unit — everything visible is captured at once — so it is only ever distilled as its frontmost window, which must positively identify itself as capturable. macOS `NSWindowSharingNone` is honoured as the direct analogue of `IsSecureEventInputEnabled`; a private/incognito window suppresses EVEN when the window server says it is shareable (K8 already refuses incognito and a screen sensor must not be the back door); a suppressed frame carries **no window title**, because "Q3 layoffs.xlsx" in a window we refused to look at is ADR-239's password-length leak wearing a different hat. **Audio** is the one sensor where the consenting party and the captured party come apart — *the person speaking may not be the person who consented* — and no gate can obtain a third party's permission, so the problem is made structural: ambient audio is **not ambient** (explicit per-session arm, checked before participants are even characterised), and a multi-party session stores nothing without an **attributable** all-party assertion — a user id, not a boolean, because a boolean is satisfiable by a stale default.

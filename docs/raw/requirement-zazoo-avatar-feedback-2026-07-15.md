User feedback on the v0 avatar, with an attached reference image (felt-plush "Zazoo crew": egg
characters with huge glossy eyes, tiny noses, thread mouths, navy suits with white collars, nub
hands, no legs, around a felt "zazoo" logo). Verbatim below.

---

I wanted the cat to align to the theme of attached image. Also I want the cat to look young and
adorable, not mature as it currently is. I want the cat to have a mediatating pose as well. I want
its whisters to move in air. When listening, its ears should enlarge (use mild exageration). Learn
from best practises of pixar studios. I want a meditating pose as well. The tail should also be
oval and not like a tube. I want the hand to be similar to that in attached image. No legs (legs
appear only when character moves). Also I want a hide pose and sneek pose. Hide pose the cat
compresses itself into its dress and only a miniature grey oval egg like object is left on screen.
The avatar's home is the camera notch of macbook and when hovered there, it should peek its head
out of the notch. I want to see that peek animation as well.

I am not happy with the detailing of the roadmap. Based on your research of disney characters,
make the cat more alive. I want eye brow animations, more vivid facial expression. Currently the
cheeks are emotionless. In the roadmap, add creation of additional avatars of major animals. Also
the cat doesnt purr, but speaks like pixar characters. Provide an option to dress up cat and
change the colour of cat.

Its nose and mouth are currently unrealistic, again look for attached image reference. I want its
breathing frequency to vary with emotions. Cheek colour to slightly reflect its emotions.

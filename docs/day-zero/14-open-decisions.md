# Open decisions

These are the smallest set of founder decisions that materially change the launch plan. They should be resolved before external pilot work expands.

## D1 — First paid workflow

Decision: DealPilot thesis → source → deal, or Relationship + Task Manager evidence → next action.

Recommendation: choose DealPilot only if the first customer can provide authorized Source access and Google OAuth during launch preparation. Otherwise use Relationship + Task Manager.

Why it matters: changes customer profile, integration work, legal language, metrics, demo, and blocker list.

## D2 — First design partner

Decision: name the actual Organization, owner, up to five users, and weekly champion.

Recommendation: one founder-accessible team with a weekly recurring workflow and permission to use outcomes as anonymized product learning.

## D3 — Pilot commercial terms

Decision: approve or revise the proposed $5,000 onboarding plus $2,500 monthly price.

Recommendation: charge from the start. Trade discounts only for prepayment, reduced support, or explicit case-study rights.

## D4 — Supported release surface

Decision: macOS version(s), web scope, and whether mobile approvals are included.

Recommendation: one or two current macOS versions plus supported browsers; mobile excluded from the first contractual scope.

## D5 — Distribution method

Decision: signed/notarized external pilot build versus a more limited supervised internal distribution path.

Recommendation: sign and notarize before handling an external customer’s private work.

## D6 — Model providers

Decision: Local models only, approved Cloud model, or mixed routing; set provider and spend caps.

Recommendation: Local default for private capture and context; explicit Cloud eligibility for approved public or authorized work; per-Organization hard budgets.

## D7 — Data topology

Decision: exact data that may enter hosted Supabase/Bridge Cloud versus remaining Local.

Recommendation: keep private operational data Local for the first pilot; host identity and explicitly authorized public-safe state only.

## D8 — Commons pilot scope

Decision: which Bridge-curated packages are available and who holds signing/publishing authority.

Recommendation: fixed allowlist, one primary and one backup key custodian, no external publishers.

## D9 — Support coverage

Decision: coverage hours, customer channel, escalation phone, and named owners.

Recommendation: business-hours founder-led support with 24/7 emergency path only for security/data incidents.

## D10 — Legal owner and jurisdiction

Decision: contracting entity, governing law, privacy jurisdictions, and counsel.

Recommendation: complete before sending customer data or installing the desktop client.

## D11 — Telemetry

Decision: which content-free operational and product Events are collected and retained.

Recommendation: minimum needed for reliability, security, cost, and agreed pilot outcomes; customer-visible policy; no raw content.

## D12 — Public positioning

Decision: Bridge master brand versus Zazoo/Avatar brand relationship.

Recommendation: Bridge is the platform and commercial product; Zazoo is an optional flagship Avatar character or public storytelling property. Avoid presenting them as competing products.

## D13 — Success threshold

Decision: one measurable workflow target and one commercial conversion threshold.

Recommendation: at least 30% cycle-time improvement, complete provenance for accepted outcomes, zero critical incidents, under two weekly support hours by day 90, and annual conversion intent.

## D14 — Stop conditions

Decision: conditions that pause the pilot.

Recommendation:

- any confirmed cross-tenant or unauthorized private-data disclosure;
- authority bypass for an External Action;
- unrecoverable data loss;
- unresolved critical package or credential compromise;
- customer refuses the agreed governance boundary;
- support burden makes safe operation impossible.

## Approval record template

For each decision record:

- decision and date;
- owner;
- rationale;
- alternatives rejected;
- consequences;
- evidence needed for reversal;
- affected contract, roadmap, and task links.

# TASK-012 VOCAB3 completion

VOCAB3 is complete under AP-055. TASK-012 remains `in_progress` for VOCAB4–VOCAB6 and final compatibility deletion.

## Delivered

- Signed pre-VOCAB3 Commons entries retain their original canonical signed content, SHA-256 content-hash pin, publication time, Ed25519 signature, trusted-key check, provenance, security scan, and dependency pins.
- A deterministic adapter projects those verified entries into canonical Organization/Module/Record manifests, including Organization vocabulary, permission resource types, Blueprint entities, views, and relation targets.
- Commons reads the current `modules/` directory and the prior registry directory. New writes stay canonical. A same-name/version collision across roots halts instead of hiding or choosing an entry.

Artifact remained 234; Helpdesk, Touchpoint, Knowledge, and Tool remain later-family work; the sole Package occurrence is the technical `packages/` path.

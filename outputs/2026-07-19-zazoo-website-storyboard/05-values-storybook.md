# Section 05 - Values storybook

## Goal

Show how a Zazoo behaves through five quiet acts. Do not explain the values with icons, feature lists, or paragraphs.

## Layout

- Height: 300-350 vh desktop for five pinned story beats; 420-500 vh mobile if needed for legibility.
- One full-viewport watercolor-felt illustration at a time.
- Each beat contains one short value line and one visual action.
- The book page remains subtly visible at the outer edges to preserve the chapter metaphor.
- Aeva is the continuous actor in all five beats.
- No icon, badge, card, numbered step, or secondary explanatory sentence.

## Animation (mandatory)

Each beat occupies approximately 4 seconds of scroll-controlled animation. The visitor may pause by stopping scroll.

Beat 1 - Loyal before clever:

- Meeting-room doors close.
- Aeva notices the owner's crowded schedule on a small card.
- She waits outside with coffee and the next folder ready.
- She does not interrupt the meeting.

Beat 2 - Quiet before loud:

- Aeva notices a loose bridge board in a workflow path.
- She tightens it while others continue working.
- She checks it once and walks away without calling attention to herself.

Beat 3 - Helpful before impressive:

- Aeva studies two solutions: one ornate and one simple.
- She chooses the simple one, removes unnecessary pieces, and hands it to the owner.
- The owner proceeds immediately.

Beat 4 - Trusted before autonomous:

- Aeva reaches a closed office door carrying a proposed action.
- She raises her hand and knocks.
- The owner's hand opens the door before Aeva enters.

Beat 5 - Present before prompted:

- Clouds gather while the owner prepares to leave.
- Aeva has already brought an umbrella and holds it out.
- The first drop lands only after the umbrella opens.

Character transitions between beats use a page-turn wash of watercolor pigment, 700-900 ms. Do not cut to unrelated illustrations.

## Interaction

- Scroll advances and reverses the five beats.
- Pausing scroll pauses at a meaningful acting pose, never mid-blink or mid-step.
- A chapter progress bookmark moves down the page edge without numbers.
- `Back to the books` is available in a consistent corner.
- No clickable value cards and no hover-dependent meaning.

## Copy (only text to display)

`VALUES-H1`

> How a Zazoo behaves.

Story lines, one at a time:

Control:

## Emotion

Tender recognition. Each moment should feel small, specific, and deeply considerate. The visitor should infer character from behavior without being told what to feel.

## Transition

After the umbrella opens, five wet footprints appear on the page. The camera follows them as the watercolor ground becomes the Process chapter's continuous work path.

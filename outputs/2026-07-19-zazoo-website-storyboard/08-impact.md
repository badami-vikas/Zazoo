# Section 08 - Impact

## Goal

Show that the value of a Zazoo belongs to the owner: time returned, friction removed, progress made, and achievement supported. Do not make the companions celebrate their own output.

## Layout

- Height: 180-220 vh desktop; 220-260 vh mobile.
- One evolving workspace seen across time, not a KPI dashboard.
- Foreground: the owner's path through meaningful work.
- Midground: Aeva and the crew quietly removing small obstacles and coordinating details.
- Background: the progress plant from the hero grows into a small tree, the notebook gains completed chapters, and the room becomes more capable without becoming cluttered.
- Final tableau: the owner is recognized at a distant stage or reaches a personally meaningful milestone; Aeva applauds quietly from backstage.
- Any quantitative impact copy occupies an editorial margin and appears only when evidence is approved.

## Animation (mandatory)

Length: 18-second scroll-linked time-lapse.

1. Morning desk: repeated small frictions appear as stones, loose papers, and disconnected paths.
2. Aeva removes or routes them one by one; the owner remains focused on the central work.
3. The crew coordinate: Lina brings evidence, Nori clears an approval, Bomi strengthens a workflow, Jia closes a relationship loop, and Neva improves the presentation.
4. Daylight advances across the room. The plant grows, but only after visible owner progress.
5. The owner's work reaches a completed form and moves to a stage/checkpoint.
6. The audience recognizes the owner. Aeva watches from backstage, gives a small proud clap, and returns to preparing what comes next.

Do not animate counters, charts, upward arrows, confetti cannons, trophies for Aeva, or a `task complete` celebration centered on the software.

## Interaction

- Scroll controls the passage of time.
- Hover/focus on the notebook briefly reveals earlier completed chapters as illustrations, not metrics.
- If approved quantitative copy is unavailable, the layout closes the editorial margin and the visual recenters automatically; do not leave placeholders.
- Omit `IMPACT-EVIDENCE` until every quantity is substantiated and approved.
- Do not display fake counters, sample metrics, or unverified customer outcomes.
- `Back to the books` remains available.

## Copy (only text to display)

`IMPACT-H1`

> Your success is your Zazoo's success.

`IMPACT-BODY`

> Quietly removing everyday friction so you can focus on extraordinary work.

`IMPACT-EVIDENCE`

> Hundreds of hours returned. Thousands of decisions coordinated. Millions of tiny hurdles removed.

Control:

> Back to the books

## Emotion

Pride without self-congratulation. The owner is the protagonist. Aeva is happiest when the owner's work advances.

## Transition

Applause fades visually into drifting evening dust. The stage light becomes sunset through the original office window. The four chapter books close on the library table as the office day ends.

# Section 06 - Process footprints

## Goal

Show the operating rhythm: insight becomes decision, delegation, execution, and learning. Make the human decision boundary visible and graceful.

## Layout

- Height: 180-220 vh desktop; 220-260 vh mobile.
- A single continuous path crosses one illustrated workspace from left to right on desktop and top to bottom on mobile.
- Tiny Aeva footprints are the path. They connect five environmental moments, not five UI icons.
- Insight: a reading desk where a pattern glows between documents.
- Decision: a fork in the path with the owner visible beyond it.
- Delegation: a shared table where Aeva passes work to Lina and Nori.
- Execution: the crew moves work through real props and spaces.
- Learning: Aeva records the result in a growing field notebook; a new path becomes easier to traverse.
- The five stage labels sit near their corresponding environment and remain secondary to the story.

## Animation (mandatory)

Length: 20-second scroll-linked sequence.

1. `Insight` - footprints form as Aeva reads, notices a pattern, and marks it.
2. `Decision` - footprints reach a fork, stop completely, and turn back toward the owner. Aeva holds the proposed path open but does not cross.
3. The owner selects the permitted branch through scroll continuation or the explicit Continue control.
4. `Delegation` - footprints resume. Aeva divides the work and hands it to the appropriate companions.
5. `Execution` - multiple smaller footprint trails work in parallel, then reconverge with completed outputs.
6. `Learning` - Aeva reviews the result, writes one lesson in her notebook, and redraws the original route with one obstacle removed.

The Decision pause is mandatory and lasts until the visitor continues. It is the only point where ambient character locomotion stops; breathing and gaze continue.

## Interaction

- Scroll moves the footprints along the path.
- At Decision, scrolling forward reveals the Continue control; activating it resumes the sequence.
- Keyboard users tab to Continue; Space/Enter activates it.
- Scrolling backward reverses the journey but does not replay the approval as a fake system event; it is a narrative scrub only.
- No form, fake approval dashboard, branching configuration, or gamified completion state.

## Copy (only text to display)

`PROCESS-H1`

## Emotion

Confidence with agency. The visitor should feel supported, never bypassed. The pause must feel respectful rather than obstructive.

## Transition

The final learned route becomes a horizontal dividing line. The page widens into a split-stage theater: Typical AI on the left and the Zazoo world on the right.

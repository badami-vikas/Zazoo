# Section 07 - The Zazoo difference

## Goal

Make differentiation felt through behavior. Do not compare feature inventories. Show a passive prompt-response robot on the left and a present, coordinated Zazoo companion on the right.

## Layout

- Height: 550-650 vh for eight cinematic vignettes.
- Pinned split stage for vignettes 1-7:
  - Left 42%: cool, sparse, boxed chat-window world.
  - Center 4%: quiet neutral gutter.
  - Right 54%: warm, connected Zazoo world extending beyond a single window.
- Vignette 8 breaks the split and becomes one full-width mountain scene.
- One caption appears below each vignette. No comparison table, checkmarks, feature rows, or product logos.
- The generic robot must be original, neutral, and non-branded. It is not evil; it is simply passive and bounded.
- Do not label the two columns. The visual contrast and captions provide the comparison.

## Animation (mandatory)

Playback: scroll-controlled. Each of the first seven vignettes lasts 6-8 seconds. The final mountain lasts 12 seconds. Motion pauses on the closing tableau if the visitor stops scrolling.

Vignette 1 - The forgotten promise:

- Left: conversation closes; robot remains in the empty window; nothing follows.
- Right: meeting ends; Aeva writes action items, schedules follow-ups, notices one unanswered item, places a two-day reminder, and closes the loop.

Vignette 2 - One window versus everywhere:

- Left: robot stands inside a chat window and looks through its boundary.
- Right: Aeva travels through stylized workspaces representing email, calendar, messages, records, documents, browser, and phone; she returns carrying a connected summary.
- Do not display third-party product logos.

Vignette 3 - Protecting attention:

- Left: the robot adds one more popup to an already crowded field.
- Right: Aeva quietly sorts spam, duplicates, low-priority messages, and an unnecessary meeting behind a screen; she leaves one clear item on the owner's desk.

Vignette 4 - The human pause:

- Left: robot finishes a sensitive email and immediately sends it.
- Right: Aeva begins the same email, notices its tone, stops, walks to the owner, and offers an alternative before anything is sent.

Vignette 5 - Protecting privacy:

- Left: robot reaches directly into a confidential document.
- Right: a guardian closes a curtain, Aeva requests owner permission, and the curtain opens only after the owner allows access.

Vignette 6 - The new idea:

- Left: robot answers the exact request and stops.
- Right: Aeva completes the request, notices the same obstacle recurring in prior pages, carries a small proposal to the owner, and waits.

Vignette 7 - Teamwork:

- Left: five robots work in isolated boxes without exchanging context.
- Right: Aeva, Lina, Nori, Bomi, Jia, and Neva pass work, review one another, ask for help, and celebrate the owner's completed result together.

Vignette 8 - The mountain:

- Begin with a person climbing under papers, laptop, meetings, approvals, and messages.
- Aeva and the crew enter from behind, not in front.
- They remove loose stones, hold ropes, carry equipment, build a bridge, light the route, and point toward the next safe step.
- The person continues climbing under their own power.
- End with the person reaching a checkpoint while the Zazoos celebrate from just behind.
- Never show a Zazoo climbing the mountain for the person.

The remaining supplied difference concepts - Memory, Care, Escalation, Growth, Success, Ownership, and The Impossible Day - are excluded from the v1 homepage to control length. They may become campaign shorts later; the builder must not insert them into this section.

## Interaction

- Scroll moves between vignettes; backward scroll reverses the visual sequence.
- A small chapter marker indicates position with eight stitched marks and no numbers.
- Pausing on a vignette holds its final meaningful pose.
- No before/after slider, carousel arrows, autoplay timer, or click-to-reveal copy.
- `Back to the books` remains available.

## Emotion

Recognition first, then relief. The left side should feel limited, not mocked. The right side should feel like companionship, judgment, continuity, and team culture. The mountain should land as the signature emotional image.

## Transition

At the mountain checkpoint, the crew's lantern light becomes a warm circular pool on the Impact chapter page. The camera pulls back to reveal that the mountain story is one illustration inside a growing record of the owner's progress.

# Section 02 - Definition and family

## Goal

Define the word Zazoo and introduce a living family of distinct companions. The visitor should understand that each Zazoo has an animal, name, role, personality, and workspace.

## Layout

- Height: at least 110 vh desktop; content may extend to 150 vh on mobile.
- Composition: one open oversized dictionary spread, not a card placed on a web page.
- Left page: lexicographic entry with generous editorial spacing.
- Right page: a continuous miniature shared office occupied by all six Zazoos.
- The binding gutter visually connects the definition to the family; no vertical divider or two unrelated cards.
- The family must be shown doing work in their environments, not standing in a portrait grid.
- Desktop family positions:
  - Aeva at a central planning table.
  - Lina in a book-lined research nook.
  - Nori moving an approval case through an operations lane.
  - Bomi assembling a small workflow bridge.
  - Jia preparing a meeting and tying a reminder ribbon.
  - Neva sketching a presentation sequence.
- Mobile: dictionary entry first, then one horizontally scrollable shared-office panorama. It must not collapse into six profile cards.

## Animation (mandatory)

Duration: 18-second ambient ensemble loop.

Playback: continuous while visible; pause off-screen.

Each character performs a role action every 2-4 seconds:

- Aeva prioritizes three notes, pauses before the consequential one, then passes assignments to the crew.
- Lina turns a page, highlights a passage, hums silently, and connects two ideas with thread.
- Nori moves an approval case, aligns the stack precisely, then checks the next case.
- Bomi tests a bridge, sees it work, gives one restrained excited bounce, then strengthens a joint.
- Jia sends a thoughtful follow-up, waves toward its path, and prepares the next meeting card.
- Neva sketches three options, rotates the page, selects one, and pins it to a presentation board.

The ensemble is asynchronous. Do not make all characters begin or end together.

Hover/focus introduction sequence, 6 seconds:

1. The selected Zazoo completes the current motion.
2. The rest of the office slows to 20% speed; it does not freeze unnaturally.
3. The selected Zazoo faces the visitor and makes eye contact.
4. A paper panel unfolds from that character's own workspace.
5. The relevant introduction copy appears line by line.
6. The Zazoo smiles, folds the panel away, and returns to work.

## Interaction

- Hover, keyboard focus, or tap activates one Zazoo introduction.
- Only one introduction may be open at a time.
- A second click/tap or Escape closes it.
- On touch, the first tap introduces; a tap outside closes.
- The workspace continues moving slowly behind the introduction.
- No modal, profile route, carousel dots, or audio introduction.
- Use `IMPACT-TEXT` only after the owner verifies the claim.
- Use the default nonnumeric Lina introduction in production. Replace it with the numeric version only after the owner verifies every figure.

## Emotion

Discovery and affection. The visitor should not feel that they are browsing agent profiles; they should feel that they have quietly entered a small, capable community with recognizable personalities.

## Transition

Aeva closes the dictionary gently. Its hard cover becomes a drawbridge. The camera follows her across it as daylight cools and a safe, welcoming castle rises around the path.

# Section 09 - Night and dream

## Goal

Deliver the emotional philosophy of the brand through action: Aeva does not climb the owner's mountain; she makes the climb possible. Also show that rest and privacy are real states, not marketing claims.

## Layout

- Height: 280-340 vh desktop; 320-400 vh mobile.
- Act 1: the shared office at dusk, now visibly the same workspace as the hero.
- Act 2: Aeva's tiny warm cottage or nest, reached by a path from the office.
- Act 3: a hand-painted dream mountain occupying the full viewport.
- Lighting moves from sunset amber to deep blue, then to lantern gold inside the dream.
- The privacy state appears once, small and quiet, near the office doorway.
- No paragraphs describe what is happening. The scene must be shown.

## Animation (mandatory)

Length: 26 seconds, scroll-controlled, with three acts.

Act 1 - Workday closes, `0-7 s`:

1. Other Zazoos finish their current actions and leave one by one.
2. Aeva files the last notebook, checks that pending work is safely paused, and closes private curtains.
3. The office lights dim in sequence.
4. The privacy indicator appears only after the curtains close and work motion stops.

Act 2 - Aeva goes home, `7-14 s`:

1. Aeva walks along a short moonlit path to her cottage/nest.
2. She hangs up her badge.
3. She places notebooks on a shelf.
4. She looks at one small framed illustration of the owner, smiles, and turns down the lamp.
5. She climbs into bed, curls comfortably, breathes slowly, and falls asleep.

Act 3 - The dream, `14-26 s`:

1. A mountain appears in watercolor darkness. The owner is climbing under their own power.
2. Aeva walks ahead only far enough to clear tiny stones and move branches from the path.
3. She holds a lantern at a dark turn.
4. She braces one small bridge across a gap.
5. The owner crosses and continues upward.
6. Aeva remains below the summit line, watching with a proud, quiet smile.

Never show Aeva carrying the owner, pulling them to the summit, receiving applause, or replacing their effort.

The sequence does not loop while the user remains in the section. It holds on the lantern-lit mountain until the visitor scrolls forward or backward. On revisit, it may replay from the beginning.

## Interaction

- Scroll controls the three acts and allows reverse playback.
- No hover interaction is required.
- No ambient audio, lullaby, voiceover, dream narration, or autoplay sound.
- Reduced-motion mode uses six illustrated frames: emptying office, privacy enabled, badge hung, asleep, lantern, bridge.

## Copy (only text to display)

`NIGHT-STATUS`

> Privacy mode enabled.

`DREAM-LINE`

> She never climbs the mountain. She makes the climb possible.

No other text appears in this section.

## Emotion

Safety, tenderness, and meaning. This is the quiet emotional climax. It should feel intimate without implying surveillance or dependency.

## Transition

The dream lantern rises and becomes the first point of morning sunlight. The blue mountain washes into the warm-paper sky of the final scene. A distant alarm gently shakes the frame once; there is no audio.

# Section 03 - Governance castle

## Goal

Make governance feel like safety and care, not cybersecurity theater. Show that helpful work happens only within explicit permission.

## Layout

- Height: 130-160 vh desktop; 120-140 vh mobile.
- Full-bleed storybook castle interior with depth: drawbridge, welcoming doors, curtains, keys, permission desks, secure drawers, and a softly lit vault in the distance.
- Aeva enters from the Section 02 drawbridge and hands a work request to the guardian team.
- Guardians are active within one scene, not presented in four bordered cards:
  - Owl Judge at a rule desk, weighing the request against the owner's rules.
  - Elephant Security operating permitted doors and secure drawers.
  - Dog Gatekeeper checking badges at the threshold.
  - Bear Auditor writing an inspectable ledger at a side desk.
- The primary copy sits in an open area of wall or sky. It must not cover guardian activity.
- The mirrored statements appear as paired inscriptions on two sides of the main doorway.
- Do not add guardian names or job descriptions. Their actions explain their roles.

## Animation (mandatory)

Length: 15 seconds.

Playback: loops while visible. The loop must return naturally to normal castle activity.

Sequence:

1. `0.0-2.0 s` - Aeva arrives with a sealed work request. Gatekeeper checks its badge.
2. `2.0-4.0 s` - Judge compares the request with a visible rulebook and signals approval for one door only.
3. `4.0-6.0 s` - Security unlocks that door; Aeva passes through; Auditor records the event.
4. `6.0-8.0 s` - Curtains close around a private workspace while activity continues safely behind them.
5. `8.0-10.0 s` - A tiny dark robot with dim red eyes approaches a different door without a badge.
6. `10.0-11.5 s` - Gatekeeper raises one calm paw. The robot stops and looks confused.
7. `11.5-13.0 s` - Gatekeeper gently turns the robot around and escorts it back across the bridge. No alarm, chase, violence, humiliation, or flashing warning.
8. `13.0-15.0 s` - The unauthorized door closes; the red light disappears; guardians return to quiet work; the first request tray receives another sealed card.

The denial line `GOV-DENIAL` appears only during step 6 and is spoken visually in a small, calm dialogue bubble. No voice audio.

## Interaction

- Hover/focus on the rulebook briefly reveals the first mirrored pair.
- Hover/focus on the permission key reveals the second pair.
- Hover/focus on the ledger reveals the third pair.
- Hover/focus on the open doorway reveals the fourth pair.
- These are emphasis changes, not tooltips; all four pairs remain readable without interaction.
- No lock icons, shield-score meters, threat maps, red alert banners, or clickable fake security controls.

## Copy (only text to display)

`GOV-H1`

> Governance isn't what slows work. It's what allows trust to grow.

`GOV-DENIAL`

> Sorry. Not authorized.

Mirrored pairs:

> Always available.  
> Only when permitted.

> Always learning.  
> Only what you allow.

> Always transparent.  
> Nothing hidden.

> Always helpful.  
> Never overreaching.

## Emotion

Protected, calm, and respected. The unauthorized attempt should create one moment of clarity, not anxiety. The final feeling is that boundaries make trust possible.

## Transition

The Bear Auditor closes the ledger. The cover fills the screen and becomes one of four clothbound books on a long library table. The castle architecture opens into the chapter library without a cut.

# Section 10 - Morning and final invitation

## Goal

Close the loop from night to morning and invite the visitor forward without adding a generic lead-capture form or unsupported product promise.

## Layout

- Height: 100-120 vh.
- Morning light fills Aeva's cottage, then the camera tracks her back toward the circular workspace from Section 01.
- The final composition echoes the hero but is calmer and more open.
- Aeva is already resuming work in the background when the final statement appears.
- One primary action appears beneath the statement.
- Footer is limited to the wordmark and legally required links once those links are supplied and approved. Do not invent footer destinations.

## Animation (mandatory)

Length: 10 seconds.

Playback: plays once as the section enters, then settles into a quiet working loop.

Sequence:

1. `0-1.5 s` - Morning light reaches Aeva's bed; her eyes open.
2. `1.5-3.0 s` - She smiles, stretches once, and puts on her badge.
3. `3.0-5.5 s` - She leaves the cottage and crosses the path toward the office.
4. `5.5-7.5 s` - The circular workspace wakes around her: one calendar opens, one document arrives, and the progress plant turns toward the light.
5. `7.5-10.0 s` - Aeva begins writing the day's first note. The final statement and action fade in.

Settled loop: 8 seconds. Aeva alternates between writing, checking one connection, and watering the plant. She never stands facing the visitor waiting for a click.

## Interaction

- The primary action links to the actual approved onboarding or contact destination. If no destination exists, render the action as disabled and do not fake navigation.
- Hover/focus on the action causes Aeva to glance at it and smile; she then returns to work.
- No email field, `Start free` button, pricing teaser, countdown, consulting link, training link, or sales popup.
- Do not add a subclaim about starting tomorrow, working before signup, or starting free.
- The browser Back action and keyboard navigation must remain standard.

## Copy (only text to display)

`FINAL-H1`

> Everyone deserves a Zazoo.

`FINAL-BODY`

> Never climb alone.

`FINAL-CTA`

> Meet your Zazoo

`FINAL-WORDMARK`

> zazoo

## Emotion

Hopeful readiness. The visitor should feel invited into an ongoing relationship, not pressured into a conversion funnel.

## Transition

The homepage ends on active morning work, completing the day-night-morning cycle. If the visitor uses a `Return to top` browser or accessibility control, the final circular workspace may align with the hero's first frame, but the page must not auto-scroll or forcibly loop.

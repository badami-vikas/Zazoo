# Marketing website platform brief — designer handoff

Full brief: [docs/raw/marketing-website-platform-brief-2026-07-18.md](../docs/raw/marketing-website-platform-brief-2026-07-18.md)

## Outcome

Wrote a standalone, non-jargon brief for the external designer building Bridge's marketing website, covering:

- What Bridge is in plain English, split into the platform story (Living Software) and the sellable flagship product (DealPilot).
- Audience (VC/PE primary, technical platform evaluators secondary).
- Positioning one-liners and five messaging pillars, ranked — governance/draft-then-approve ranked as the top differentiator, not a buried trust-page footnote.
- Voice/tone rules and a banned-vocabulary list (no CRM terms — Lead/Pipeline/Contact-as-noun/Prospect).
- Visual system handed over as data: color tokens, type scale, spacing/radius/motion, sourced from the existing product design system (`design-system.md` / prototype `theme.css`) so the marketing site stays one brand family with the product.
- An explicit avoid-list (generic AI-hero SaaS look, fabricated metrics/logos, meeting-bot/gamification imagery, anything implying silent inbox scraping).
- Proposed site map and homepage section-by-section brief.
- Three open questions flagged for the founder, not guessed at: final one-liner, CTA model (request-access vs. self-serve pricing — team pilot stage, no GA yet), and whether v1 ships DealPilot-only or includes the multi-vertical platform story.

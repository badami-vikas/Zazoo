# Alternative homepage: Section Design Decisions

Experiment route: `/alternative-home.html` (isolated Vite entry; `/` untouched).
Visual language: inherited Zazoo tokens (paper, parchment, ink, steel, sage, amber; Source Serif 4 display + Geist body) and the site's CSS-illustrated, sticky-scene, scroll-progress idiom. One light warm theme for the whole page; section backgrounds shift within the same family so no hard white gap ever appears. One shared motif carries the whole film: an "organization form", a cluster of people, rooms, and pathways, that is rigid in Section 1, comes alive in Section 2, is entered in Section 3, and evolves through five states in Section 4.

## Section 1: Patterns across paradigm shifts

Entering emotion: recognition ("I remember these organizations and eras") -> exiting emotion: reflective urgency ("the question is how we redesign, not how we adopt"). Misconception challenged: AI is another technology adoption cycle. Discovery mechanism: a pinned comparative stage repeats one visual grammar across five eras. On the left, the same rigid organization form appears in every era with the era's technology bolted onto its edge; on the right, the organization is reshaped around the technology. The visitor watches the identical pattern recur (steam, electricity, computing, internet) before any lesson is stated, so repetition, not prose, reveals the rule; the AI era then reuses the left half but leaves the right half as unfinished blueprint lines, which makes the missing answer the point. Chosen medium: a single full-viewport two-column stage advanced by page scroll (evidence morphs in place, 600-900 ms crossfades; era changes crossfade, never slide), because the argument is literally "the same thing keeps happening", and only a fixed stage with changing evidence can show sameness; a timeline or card grid would present eras as separate stories and destroy the repetition that carries the insight.

## Section 2: The AI-native organization

Entering emotion: curiosity ("what would actually be different?") -> exiting emotion: possibility ("this changes the organization, not only its tools"). Misconception challenged: adopting AI means adding tools to existing workflows. Discovery mechanism: the unfinished blueprint from Section 1 resolves into one living organization drawn in a recognizable but fragmented state: knowledge sits with individuals, department boundaries wall teams apart, decisions travel up the hierarchy and back down, repeated work restarts from zero. Scroll (or four direct controls) transforms one relationship at a time, knowledge flows, decisions shorten, capability compounds, memory persists, and the visitor watches consequences propagate through the same people before any conclusion is worded; the approved lines appear only after their visual change has landed. Chosen medium: a morphing before/after organizational illustration with a four-state scrubber plus a "Compare before" reset, because the claim is structural, and a structure must be seen changing; an org chart or capability diagram would restate the misconception (boxes with AI added), while a living illustration shows relationships, the thing that actually changes, moving.

## Section 3: Business AI Infrastructure

Entering emotion: familiar confidence ("this task should be simple") -> exiting emotion: relief and recognition ("the environment created the friction, not the work"). Misconception challenged: AI transformation mainly requires better software, models, integrations, or automation. Discovery mechanism: the camera enters the organization and follows one employee through one task, "Prepare the board presentation", twice. First pass: six familiar questions surface from the environment one at a time, each visibly bending the work path into detours, waits, and duplicates while the employee stays calm. Then the surrounding environment, never the employee, the task, or any technology, changes: knowledge moves into reach, ownership and approval paths become visible, prior work surfaces, colleagues and quiet AI companions appear within boundaries. The identical task replays without friction, then the camera pulls back to hundreds of calm work paths. Because only one variable changed between the two passes, the visitor deduces the environment is the answer before the definition is shown. Chosen medium: a scroll-controlled animated story with the six questions as real buttons that trace their detour and frictionless route, because a controlled experiment (same task, changed environment) can only be communicated by watching both runs; any prose or diagram would have to assert the conclusion the replay lets the visitor discover.

## Section 4: Managing intelligence

Entering emotion: orientation ("where are we?") -> exiting emotion: constructive urgency ("the next state requires intentional organizational redesign"). Misconception challenged: organizations progress mainly by deploying more AI. Discovery mechanism: the coordinated work paths from Section 3 gather into one continuous path holding five versions of the same organization. Only one state is expanded at a time; the illustration changes the organization itself across states (tools in hands, redesigned workflows, shared reusable knowledge, changed governance and roles, finally a form competitors cannot copy) while the visible amount of "AI" never grows, which quietly falsifies the more-deployment assumption. Four question lenses re-light the same illustration (what changes, where value comes from, how leadership evolves, what becomes possible next) with the approved stage description as the only prose, and leadership figures become more consequential at every state. Chosen medium: an interactive transformation journey on one path, examined through lenses, because executives arrive asking "where are we?", and recognition requires comparing qualitatively different environments of one's own organization; a maturity scale or score would turn recognition into grading and imply progress by accumulation, exactly the misconception the section exists to break.

## Animation register (duration, trigger, playback, loop, resting frame)

- S1 evidence morph: 700 ms ease crossfade per evidence step; trigger: page scroll steps inside a pinned ~600 vh (desktop) / ~380 vh (mobile) range; playback scrubbed and fully reversible; no loop; resting frame: the unfinished AI-era organization with one open pathway leading into Section 2.
- S1 era switch: 700 ms crossfade/morph; trigger: era selector (click, tap, keyboard); no loop; selected era preserved when scrolling backward.
- S2 transformation: 18-24 s equivalent scroll-linked sequence (six beats); trigger: scroll plus four direct controls and "Compare before"; scrubbed, reversible; no loop; resting frame: intelligence moving as a calm shared current, drawn as slow paper-toned flows, not a glowing network.
- S3 story: 24-30 s equivalent scroll range across five scenes; trigger: page scroll; question buttons re-trace one detour (first pass) and its frictionless route (replay); scrubbed, reversible; no loop; resting frame: many calm work paths over one shared environment.
- S4 stage transition: 850 ms morph of the same organization between states; trigger: stage selection (click, tap, arrow keys, focus + Enter); hover previews but is never required; no loop; resting frame: the selected stage visibly connected to the next state.
- Reduced motion: every sequence collapses to meaningful still frames per step with 240 ms crossfades; continuous drifts and travel animations are disabled; the full argument (all steps, all states, all copy) remains reachable by scroll and controls.
- All off-screen scenes pause: scene work is gated on the IntersectionObserver `active` flag from the site's shared `useSceneProgress` hook.

## Deliberate limitations (recorded)

- "Assess your organization" has no approved destination; it renders visibly unavailable (same honest `aria-disabled` treatment as the existing homepage's final invitation).
- The existing site has no approved consulting CTA component, so Section 4 ends on the selected transformation state; the missing final CTA is recorded in the handoff.
- Section 3's six question reveals are visual only; no explanatory microcopy is approved, and the missing optional microcopy is recorded in the handoff.
